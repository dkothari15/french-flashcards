Level path

A practical overview of what each level usually emphasizes.

A1

Survival grammar: articles, être/avoir, present tense, basic questions, simple negation, time, family, daily life.

A2

Past and future basics, pronouns, quantities, comparisons, simple relatives, obligation, routine descriptions.

B1

Independence: opinions, narration, conditional, subjunctive basics, clearer connectors, reported speech.

B2

Argumentation: nuance, concession, complex relative pronouns, passive, advanced conditionals, formal connectors.

C1

Precision: register, nominalization, discourse flow, advanced subjunctive, complex written structures.

C2

Mastery: literary recognition, rhetorical turns, legal/formal nuance, highly flexible syntax and style.
