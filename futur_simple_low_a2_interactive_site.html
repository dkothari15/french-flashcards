1. What is futur simple?

Le futur simple means the simple future tense. It is used to say what will happen.

Demain, je travaillerai.

Tomorrow, I will work.

Un jour, je parlerai très bien français.

One day, I will speak French very well.

Low A2 idea: Futur simple is often more formal, more certain, or more “future prediction” than futur proche.

The main formula

For most verbs, take the infinitive and add the future endings.

infinitive + ai, as, a, ons, ez, ont
SubjectEndingExample: parlerEnglish
je-aije parleraiI will speak
tu-astu parlerasyou will speak
il / elle / on-aelle parlerashe will speak
nous-onsnous parleronswe will speak
vous-ezvous parlerezyou will speak
ils / elles-ontils parlerontthey will speak
Memory trick: The endings are similar to avoir: ai, as, a, avons, avez, ont. For futur simple, remember nous = -ons and vous = -ez.

2. Formation: regular verbs

There are three regular patterns.

-ER verbs

Keep the whole infinitive.

parler + ending

Je parlerai.

I will speak.

-IR verbs

Keep the whole infinitive.

-RE verbs

Remove final e, then add ending.

vendre → vendr + ending

Je vendrai.

I will sell.

Negation

Put ne...pas around the future verb.

Je ne travaillerai pas demain.

I will not work tomorrow.

Nous ne sortirons pas ce soir.

We will not go out tonight.

Questions

At low A2, use Est-ce que...

Est-ce que tu travailleras demain ?

Will you work tomorrow?

Quand est-ce que vous partirez ?

When will you leave?

4. When to use futur simple

1. Future plans or events

Demain, je visiterai ma famille.

Tomorrow, I will visit my family.

2. Predictions

Il fera froid demain.

It will be cold tomorrow.

3. Promises

Je t’aiderai.

I will help you.

4. Formal future statements

Le cours commencera à neuf heures.

The class will begin at nine o’clock.

5. Weather forecasts

Il pleuvra demain.

It will rain tomorrow.

5. Important exceptions and traps

Trap 1: After “si”, do NOT use futur simple

For a real future condition, use si + present, then futur simple.

Incorrect: Si j’aurai le temps, je sortirai.

Wrong in French.

Correct: Si j’ai le temps, je sortirai.

If I have time, I will go out.

Trap 2: After “quand”, French often uses futur simple

Quand j’arriverai, je t’appellerai.

When I arrive, I will call you.

Trap 3: -RE verbs drop final e

vendre → je vendrai

to sell → I will sell

prendre → je prendrai

to take → I will take

Trap 4: Je serai vs je serais
FrenchTenseMeaning
je seraifutur simpleI will be
je seraisconditionalI would be
At low A2, focus on je serai = I will be.

Quick checklist

  1. Regular -ER/-IR verbs keep the infinitive: parlerai, finirai.
  2. Regular -RE verbs drop final e: vendre → vendrai.
  3. Irregular stems keep normal endings: serai, irai, ferai.
  4. After si, use present: Si j’ai le temps, je sortirai.
  5. After quand for a future event, French often uses future: Quand j’arriverai...
  6. Il faudra = it will be necessary / we will have to.
  7. Il pleuvra = it will rain.
